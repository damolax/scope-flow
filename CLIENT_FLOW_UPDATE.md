# ScopeFlow 6 client flow

1. The client opens a secure proposal or additional-work link without creating an account.
2. Included services are clearly separated from optional extras.
3. The client can add/remove allowed extras, change permitted quantities and request different prices.
4. A final review screen shows the exact selection, incentive and total before approval.
5. Approval creates a locked agreement snapshot and downloadable PDF.
6. Each invoice has a separate read-only secure link and PDF.
7. The client may report payment, but the business owner must confirm it.
8. Confirmed payment starts or schedules the delivery countdown according to the proposal rule.
9. The same secure project page shows status, time remaining, expected delivery and calendar buttons.
10. Pause, schedule-change, review and delivery emails keep the client informed.
